What we do

Suwanee Express Plumbing is the 24/7 line for plumbing trouble in Suwanee, GA and the towns around it. Callers reach us for clogged drains, water heaters that quit, leaks, burst pipes, sewer backups and planned work such as installations and repipes.

The work is done by licensed and insured plumbers, and same-day appointments are offered when they are available. You hear a clear price before any work starts, which is the rule the rest of the business follows.

How a call works

You call (770) 214-4545 at any hour. We ask where the problem is, what you have already shut off and whether anyone is at risk, then tell you what to do while you wait.

If the situation involves a gas smell, the answer is different. Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber, because a plumber cannot make a gas leak safe from the sidewalk.

How to check any plumber before you hire

This advice applies to us and to anyone else you call. Ask who will be at your door, ask for the price before work starts, and ask whether the job needs a permit.

You can verify a plumber's license at the Georgia Secretary of State license verification site, verify.sos.ga.gov. In Suwanee, the city's building permits page lists plumbing changes and water heater replacement as work that needs a permit, and the inspections office is at 770-945-8996.

What a plumbing visit looks like from start to finish

Whether the call is an overflowing toilet at midnight or a planned water heater swap, the visit follows the same order so nothing is a surprise.

  1. The phone call

    You describe the problem, the address and what you have already shut off. We give you the first safe steps to take while you wait.

  2. The price

    You hear a clear price before work starts. If the plumber finds something different on site, the new price is stated before that extra work begins.

  3. The work

    The plumber fixes the problem you called about and tests it. Water is run, drains are flushed and fixtures are checked before the visit ends.

  4. The follow-up advice

    You are told what caused the problem and whether anything else is close to failing, so the next decision is yours to make.

What we will and will not claim

This business does not publish review counts, star ratings or years-in-business figures, because a number on a plumbing site is only worth something when it is real and checkable. What we do state are working facts: the line is staffed 24/7, same-day appointments are offered when available, and the plumbers are licensed and insured.

If a claim on this site ever looks too good, check it. Ask for the plumber's license, ask what the price covers, and ask what happens if the job turns out bigger than described. A good answer to those three questions is worth more than any badge on a web page.

Water and drain basics every Suwanee homeowner can use

Know where your main water shutoff is before you need it. In many homes it sits near the water meter or where the line enters the house, and a ball valve closes with a quarter turn of the handle.

Know where the sewer cleanout is too. According to Gwinnett County Water Resources, the property owner maintains the private sewer lateral, and a cleanout gives a plumber a direct way into the line when a drain backs up.

Keep the numbers handy. Gwinnett County Water Resources lists 678.376.7000 as its 24/7 dispatch line for water and sewer emergencies, and Georgia 811 handles utility locates before any digging.

Jobs we are called for most

Clogged drains, water heaters that stop heating and slow leaks make up most of the calls. Drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection are the pages to read first if one of those sounds like your situation.

Larger jobs follow from the same calls. A water heater past repair becomes a replacement, and a line that keeps backing up becomes a sewer camera inspection before anyone talks about digging.
