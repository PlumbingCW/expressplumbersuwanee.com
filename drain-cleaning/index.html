When a clogged drain is an emergency

If sewage or gray water is coming up through a floor drain, tub or toilet, call the 24/7 line now. Our emergency drain cleaning in Suwanee, GA runs at night and on weekends, and the call is the same as it is at noon.

While you wait, stop running water anywhere in the house. Every flush, load of laundry and dishwasher cycle pushes more water toward the drain that is already full.

Drain cleaning is the removal of whatever is blocking a drain line, from a hair clog in a tub trap to grease and roots in the main sewer line. The right method depends on where the blockage sits, which is why the first few questions on the phone matter.

  1. Stop all water use, including the dishwasher and washing machine.
  2. Keep children and pets out of any area where sewage has surfaced.
  3. Move rugs, boxes and electronics off floors near the backed-up drain.
  4. Skip chemical drain openers. They sit in the pipe, burn skin on contact and make the next repair more dangerous for whoever reaches in.

One slow drain or the whole house? How to tell

A single slow sink usually means a clog in that fixture or its branch line. When several drains act up together, the blockage is probably lower in the system.

Gurgling after a flush

Air pushed back through other fixtures suggests the main line is restricted, not the toilet itself.

Tub fills when you run the washer

Water from the washing machine has nowhere to go and takes the lowest open path, which is often the tub.

Water at the floor drain

A basement or garage floor drain that rises with laundry day points to the main line.

One fixture only

A sink or shower that drains slowly while everything else runs normally is usually a local clog the snake can reach.

How we clear a drain, from simple to serious

Not every clog needs the biggest machine. We start with the least invasive tool that has a real chance of working, and move up only when the symptoms say so.

Hand and cable augers

A cable auger is a flexible steel cable that spins through a drain and either breaks a clog apart or hooks it for removal. It handles hair, soap buildup and the typical kitchen clog on a branch line.

Cables punch a hole through a blockage. They rarely scrape the pipe walls clean, so a drain can open and clog again within weeks.

Hydro jetting for buildup

When grease or scale coats the pipe wall, a water jet scours the full circumference. Our hydro jetting service is the answer for repeat clogs and for restaurant-style grease lines.

Camera inspection when the cause is unclear

A drain camera shows a bellied pipe, a cracked joint or a root intrusion that no cable can fix. If the same line has been cleared twice, a sewer camera inspection tells you whether to keep clearing or start repairing.

What Suwanee homeowners should know about who owns the clog

Gwinnett County Water Resources states that the property owner maintains the private sewer lateral, the pipe that runs from the house to the connection at the edge of the right-of-way. The county page on fats, oil and grease adds that if a clog happens on your property, taking care of it is your responsibility.

The county maintains the cleanout at the right-of-way, and a second cleanout closer to the house belongs to the homeowner. Knowing where both sit saves time on a service call and tells you whether a backup is yours to fix.

If sewage is backing up and your neighbors have the same problem, the blockage may be in the county main. Gwinnett County Water Resources lists 678.376.7000 as its 24/7 dispatch line for water and sewer emergencies. Call them first if the street main is the likely cause.

What the visit looks like

Each call follows the same order, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Describe the symptoms

    Which drains are slow, when it started and whether sewage has surfaced. Those answers tell us which tool to bring.

  2. Find the cleanout

    The technician locates the nearest access point, often outside near the foundation or at the property edge.

  3. Clear the line

    Cable first for ordinary clogs, jetting when the pipe wall is coated or roots are suspected.

  4. Confirm it flows

    Water is run through the drains again, and a camera pass is offered when the same clog has returned.

What changes the price of drain cleaning

A kitchen sink clog and a main line full of roots are different jobs, and the price reflects that. You hear the number before work starts.

Where the clog sits

A fixture trap is quicker than a main line that needs a cleanout and a longer cable.

The method

A cable run costs less than a jetting visit, and a camera pass is added when the cause is unclear.

Access

A clear outdoor cleanout is faster than a line that must be reached through a toilet flange.

Time of day

An overnight backup call can be priced differently from a drain visit booked for tomorrow afternoon.

Repeat clogs

A line that has clogged before may need jetting or a camera pass, not another cable run.

Home insurance usually covers sudden water damage but often excludes the cost of clearing a clog, and sewer backup coverage is commonly an add-on. Read the policy, and ask your agent before you need it.
