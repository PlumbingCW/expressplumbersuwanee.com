Inside Suwanee

Suwanee has about 20,786 residents per the 2020 Census, and the city's own plan describes Old Town as its historic downtown. Buford Highway runs through the middle of that area, so a call from near Town Center Park or Old Town is a short trip from the base.

Newer areas matter too. The plan counts Suwanee Station and Three Bridges as mixed-use developments, and it says 60 percent of all housing units date from 2000 or later. That means water heaters and supply lines in many Suwanee homes are still in their first or second decade, which is useful to know when you describe the problem.

Suwanee Creek floods often enough that the Gwinnett hazard plan calls it out, and Hurricane Helene closed Martin Farm Road in 2024. If your home sits low near the creek, mention it on the call, because a basement or sewer backup there is handled differently from a plain clog. The drain cleaning page explains the usual first steps.

ZIP codes we dispatch to

Suwanee is ZIP 30024. Duluth uses 30096 and the Gwinnett side of 30097, Buford uses 30518 and 30519, Lawrenceville uses 30046, 30043, 30044 and 30045, and Lilburn uses 30047.

ZIP codes and city limits do not always line up. ZIP 30097 spans Fulton, Gwinnett and Forsyth counties, and many addresses with a Lawrenceville mailing address sit outside the city. Give the street address on the phone and we will confirm it is a place we can reach.

What happens when you call from outside Suwanee

The call is the same wherever you are, with one extra question about the address.

  1. Give the address

    Street, town and ZIP, so dispatch can place it. Mention the county if the ZIP crosses a line.

  2. Describe the problem

    Where the water or backup is and what you already shut off. Gas smells mean leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.

  3. Hear the price

    You get a clear price before work starts, based on what you described.

  4. Book the visit

    Same-day appointments are offered when they are available, and the line stays open if things get worse.

Which utility do you call first?

Gwinnett County Water Resources lists 678.376.7000 as its 24/7 dispatch line for water and sewer emergencies. Duluth and Lilburn customers generally use the county, and Lawrenceville's water distribution moved to the county on December 1, 2020.

Buford runs its own water, sewer and gas for various areas, so a Buford caller should check which utility serves the address. If the problem is inside the house, such as a failed water heater or a burst supply line, it is a plumbing call. See Buford and Duluth for the details by town.
