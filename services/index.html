Start with what you are seeing

Most homeowners do not know the name of the job, only the symptom. Water standing in a sink points to drain cleaning, a cold shower points to water heater repair, and a wet spot that keeps growing points to leak detection.

Planned work sits on the same line as emergencies. A water heater installation, a repipe or new fixtures through plumbing installation can be booked for a day that suits you, and the price is stated before work starts.

All plumbing services

Water heaters

Water Heater Repair

No hot water, pilot light trouble, leaking tanks and noisy elements, for gas, electric and tankless units.

Water Heater Replacement

A failed or leaking tank swapped for a correctly sized unit, with the old one hauled away.

Water Heater Installation

Planned installs for new builds, upgrades and moves from gas to electric or the other way around.

Tankless Water Heater

Tankless installation, descaling flushes and repairs, including gas line and venting questions.

Drains and sewer

Drain Cleaning

Clogged sinks, tubs, floor drains and slow main lines cleared with snakes and cameras, not harsh chemicals.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water that scours grease, scale and roots from lines a snake only punches through.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Main line backups cleared at the cleanout, with a camera look afterward when the cause is unclear.

Sewer Line Repair

Broken, bellied or root-split sewer lines repaired by spot dig, trenchless lining or replacement.

Sewer Inspection

A camera run through the main line with a clear answer on what is down there and what it needs.

Leaks and pipes

Leak Detection

Hidden leaks, mystery water bills and spinning meters traced to the pipe, not guessed at.

Slab Leak Repair

Leaks under the concrete floor located and fixed by spot repair, reroute or repipe.

Burst Pipe Repair

Burst or split pipes stopped, dried out and repaired once the water is shut off.

Water Line Repair

The line from the meter to the house, repaired or replaced when pressure drops or the yard turns soggy.

Repiping

Whole-house or partial repipes for failing, corroded or leak-prone supply lines.

Fixtures and more

Toilet Repair

Running, clogged, rocking and leaking toilets repaired, plus replacements and new installs.

Faucet Repair

Drips, weak flow and valves that will not shut off, repaired or replaced at the fixture.

Garbage Disposal Repair

Jammed, humming, leaking or dead disposals repaired, or replaced and installed.

Plumbing Installation

New fixtures, lines and appliance hookups for remodels, additions and new construction.

Commercial Plumbing

Restroom, kitchen and building plumbing for shops, offices and restaurants, including after-hours calls.

Emergency or planned, the same phone line

Services marked with an emergency page cover burst pipes, backups, failed water heaters and similar problems at any hour, including nights, weekends and holidays. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first, then call a plumber once you are safe.

Not sure which page fits? Call (770) 214-4545 and describe the problem. We will tell you whether it is an emergency, a same-day job or something that can wait for a booked visit.

According to Gwinnett County Water Resources, the property owner is responsible for the private sewer lateral and for everything on the customer's side of the water meter. That is a useful dividing line when you choose which page to read.
