A house built in 1988 is now 38 years old

Census estimates (ACS 2020 to 2024) put the median year built for Lilburn homes at 1988. Of roughly 5,300 housing units, about 30 percent went up in the 1980s and another 24 percent in the 1990s. About 26 percent are older than 1980.

Age alone does not mean bad pipes, and we will not guess at what is behind your walls. Years of trade work do tell us which parts wear out on that schedule: tanks, rubber seals, wax rings, stiff shutoff valves and worn cartridges.

The city's 2024 plan records owner-occupied homes falling while rentals rose between 2010 and 2020. For a landlord or tenant that raises a plain question. Who calls the plumber at 2 a.m.? Settle it before the leak.

What tends to give out first in a house this age

Water at the base of the tank

A heater that has run well over a decade and shows rust or a damp pan is near the end. Water heater replacement starts with shutting off the supply.

A shutoff valve that will not close

Old valves under sinks seize or weep. If one fails in an emergency, shut the main and say so on the call.

A toilet that runs or stains the ceiling below

Fill valves and wax rings age out, and toilet repair costs less than the drywall that follows a seep.

A spray at a spigot or supply line

A split braided line puts out a lot of water fast. That is a main valve and a burst pipe repair call.

Gurgling in several fixtures

Grease, wipes and roots narrow a main line. Drain cleaning clears it, and a camera shows whether the pipe is the problem.

Before We Get to Your Lilburn Home

Find the main shutoff now

In a 1980s or 1990s house the main valve can be stiff. Turn it closed and open once, and tell the dispatcher if it will not move.

Clear a path to the water heater

Tanks sit behind storage in garages and closets. Move boxes back about three feet so the tank and shutoff are reachable.

Locate the sewer cleanout

Look outside near the foundation and the lot edge. Brush back leaves, but do not dig without calling 811.

Lift what water can reach

Roll up rugs and lift electronics off wet floors, and keep children and pets out of the room.

Arrange access if you rent or manage

Have a key, lockbox code or an adult on site, and say whether the owner approved the work.
