Gas safety notice

Gas leaks can cause fires, explosions and injury. If you smell gas, do the following and do not wait for a plumber.

  • Leave the building at once and take everyone with you.
  • Do not use switches, lighters, appliances or phones inside the building.
  • From a safe distance, call the gas utility, for most homes Atlanta Gas Light at 877.427.4321, or 911.
  • Call a plumber only after the gas utility or emergency responders say it is safe.
